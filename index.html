How AI agents helped us build THRIVE-Belize

Mario Morales, Aimee Slagle, and Gabriela Valdez · University of Arizona · with Toledo Community College and Hillside Health Care, Belize

Poster session. Monday, November 2, 2026, 2:30-3:30 p.m. Central Time, Exhibit Hall 3-4, Row 30. APHA 2026 Annual Meeting and Expo, San Antonio, TX.

In brief. In January and February 2026, AI agents read 38 free life-skills manuals, 5,074 pages in all, and catalogued more than 1,100 activities, each with a quote and a page number. Aimee Slagle checked random samples against the originals, and Mario Morales and Aimee Slagle approved each module. The result is 95 activities in seven modules and 31 sessions of 40 minutes.

AI tools assisted in preparing the poster and this video, including its synthetic narration. The authors verified all content. Contact: mariomorales@arizona.edu
