## The work in brief

Many good life-skills manuals are free online, but some run to 400 or 500 pages, too many for a small team to read. We asked whether AI agents could help researchers find, read, and choose activities from these manuals to build a curriculum for one school in the Toledo District of Belize. We did this work in January and February 2026.

- **Find.** In January 2026, an AI agent ran 27 documented web searches for free manuals for grades 6-12 with real classroom activities, and found 60 records.
- **Read.** Following written rules, the agents read 38 manuals in full (5,074 pages). They cut each PDF into 10-page pieces and recorded every activity in 17 fields, with a quote from the manual and its page number. In all, they catalogued more than 1,100 activities.
- **Check.** In the first manuals, Aimee Slagle compared a random 20% of the activities with the original manuals. Her checks found activities the agents had skipped or partly invented, so we required the agents to extract every activity and to show that three counts matched.
- **Choose.** The agents ranked activities by fit to the school, and Mario Morales and Aimee Slagle approved each module.

The result is a manual with **95 activities** in **seven modules** and **31 sessions of 40 minutes**, all paper-based and low-cost:

| Module | Topic | Sessions |
|---|---|---|
| M01 | Communication and emotions | 2-5 |
| M02 | Gender norms and boys' health | 6-9 |
| M03 | Sexual and reproductive health | 10-13 |
| M04 | Healthy relationships | 14-17 |
| M05 | Mental and physical health | 18-21 |
| M06 | Substance use and refusal | 22-25 |
| M07 | Environment and climate | 26-29 |

Session 1 opens the program, session 30 closes it, and a booster session follows 8-12 weeks later. We completed Phase 1, a feasibility and acceptability study at TCC, in July and August 2026.

**Who did what.** Mario Morales designed the AI workflow, wrote its rules, set the inclusion criteria, and, with Aimee Slagle, approved each module. Aimee Slagle checked random samples against the manuals and advised on cultural adaptation. Gabriela Valdez, co-principal investigator, provided institutional support.

## How AI was used

AI agents searched the web for manuals, extracted their activities, and drafted manual content under our written rules in January and February 2026. We checked their work against the original manuals and approved every module. AI tools also assisted in preparing the poster and the video, including the video's synthetic narration. The authors verified all content and take full responsibility for it.
